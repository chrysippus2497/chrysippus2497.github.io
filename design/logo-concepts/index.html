RA / INTERLOCKING STUDIES — ROUND 2

Two initials. One construction.

Three different ways to carry the R’s diagonal into the A. Each has one square connection point built into a structural joint. Enlarged views show the geometry; header and tab samples show the real size. Use 100% browser zoom.

Recommendation: Relay. The open V-shaped connection keeps both initials distinct while making them feel drawn together. Forge is stronger at tiny sizes; Keystone is the most compact and abstract.
